      How matching works
      • Skills are matched by name, with common synonyms grouped together. For example, "PowerShell" and "Python" count as scripting, and "EMR" counts as EHR.
      • Job fit = required skills your program teaches ÷ all required skills for that job.
      • Overall fit weights each job by its openings when you enter them. Otherwise every job counts equally.
      • Gaps rank by openings that need the skill, then by number of jobs.
      • Posting detection looks for about 100 common skills. Review what it finds and add anything it missed.
