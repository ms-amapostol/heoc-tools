How scoring works
  • Each answer scores 0 (Not yet) to 3 (Strong). Each area's score is its points as a share of the points possible.
  • Your overall score averages every question you've answered. Skipped questions don't count against you.
  • Stages: Exploring (0–34), Building foundations (35–59), Ready to pilot (60–79), Ready to scale (80–100).
  • Next moves start with your lowest-scoring areas. Ties go to guardrails and leadership first, since every later step depends on them.
