How the estimates work
  • Hours per year = times per week × minutes each × working weeks ÷ 60.
  • Hours you could save depends on how predictable the steps are: same steps every time 80%, mostly the same 50%, needs judgment 25%.
  • Ease (0–100) starts from how predictable the steps are, then drops for sensitive data and adjusts for the type of task. Reminders and scheduling are usually easiest; document review is hardest.
  • Quick win: 100+ hours saved and ease 55+. Big bet: 100+ hours, harder to do. Fill-in: easy, smaller payoff. Later: harder and smaller.
  • Full-time equivalent uses 2,080 hours per year.
