          Set up your program

          Coming back for a new term? On the same computer, use Plan another term on the welcome screen. It keeps your setup and moves the term forward. On a different computer, load last term's term file in the second box below.

          Turn your catalog into a program file with AI

          The fastest way to set up. Your AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot) reads your catalog, course plans and room list and writes a program file this page can read. This part happens in your AI tool's chat, in a separate tab.

          1. Copy the prompt with the button below.
          2. Open your AI tool in a new tab and paste the prompt into a new chat.
          3. Give it your information in the same chat: attach your catalog PDF and course plans (paperclip or + button), paste links, or paste the text. Add your room list and time blocks if you have them. Anything it can't find, it leaves blank for you to fill in here.
          4. Send it. The AI replies with a block of text that starts with {.
          5. Copy the AI's whole reply, paste it into the box below, and click Import. You'll review every tab before anything is used.

          Change many courses at once

          For settings that repeat. For example: every lecture course has a maximum of 35, or every lab runs Fall and Spring.

          Each plan version lists the courses a student takes at each step of the program, in order. Step 1 is a student's first term. Separate course codes with commas. Most programs have at least a full-time and a part-time version.

          Add many rooms at once

          Skip the typing if you have a room list anywhere: a facilities spreadsheet, a PDF, even a screenshot of a building schedule.

          Paste or upload a list

          One room per line: room, type, seats. Types: Lecture, Computer lab, Science lab, Clinical lab, Shop or studio, Other. A header line is fine.

          Have AI make the list from whatever you have
          1. Copy the prompt with the button below.
          2. Open your AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot) in a new tab and paste the prompt into a new chat.
          3. Attach whatever you have in that chat: a facilities spreadsheet, a PDF room list, a screenshot of a room schedule, or pasted text.
          4. Send it. The AI replies with lines like B-101,Lecture,40.
          5. Copy the AI's whole reply, open "Paste or upload a list" above, paste it in, and click Add or Replace.
