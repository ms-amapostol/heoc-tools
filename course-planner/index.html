          Turn your catalog into a program file with AI

          The fastest way to set up. Your AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot) reads your catalog, course plans and room list and writes a program file this page can read. This part happens in your AI tool's chat, in a separate tab.

          1. Copy the prompt with the button below.
          2. Open your AI tool in a new tab and paste the prompt into a new chat.
          3. Give it your information in the same chat: attach your catalog PDF and course plans (paperclip or + button), paste links, or paste the text. Add your room list and time blocks if you have them. Anything it can't find, it leaves blank for you to fill in here.
          4. Send it. The AI replies with a block of text that starts with {.
          5. Copy the AI's whole reply, paste it into the box below, and click Import. You'll review every tab before anything is used.

          Registration pace

          Upload your registration counts each week to see which sections are filling and which are at risk.

          Add this week's registration counts

          1. Run your registration report in your student system, with one row per section.
          2. Keep three columns: course code, section number, number registered. Save it as a CSV file, or copy those three columns.
          3. Optional: add last year's numbers for each section, in two more columns: last_year_same_week (registered at this week last year) and last_year_final (last year's final enrollment). Names like ly_week or prior_year_final work too. We add them up by course and show the change from last year. Projected final = last year's final × (this year's count ÷ last year's count at this week), and the flags use it. Without these columns, the flags compare today's count with your seats.
          4. Choose the file or paste the rows below, pick the week, and click Add.
